WEBSITE PRIVACY POLICYLast updated: 20 November 2026

1. Introduction

Nitro Apex Studio ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes the information practices relating to our website at nitroapexstudio.site.

2. Information we collect

This website is a static informational website. We do not intentionally operate an account system or collect personal information through a server-side form. If you choose to contact us using the contact page, your email application will prepare a message for you to send. The information in that message is handled by your email provider and the recipient of the email.

Our hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process technical information such as IP address, browser details, and request logs as part of providing and securing the website. Please consult GitHub's own privacy information for details of its practices.

3. Cookies and analytics

This website does not intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies, and no third-party analytics tool is included in this site template. If analytics, advertising, embedded media, or other third-party services are added in the future, this policy should be updated to explain their use.

4. How information is used

If you email us, we may use the information you provide to respond to your enquiry, provide support, or consider your feedback. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, or other sensitive information by email.

5. Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information collected through this website. Emails you send to us may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to handle your enquiry, maintain business records, or meet legal obligations. Your email provider may process and retain your information under its own terms.

6. Children's privacy

This website is intended for a general audience and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us so we can review the matter.

7. External links

This website may link to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those third parties. Review their policies before providing information.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you. To make a privacy-related enquiry, contact us using the details on our Contact page.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website or practices change. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact

For questions about this policy, please visit our Contact page.
